This website policy is a project-ready draft based on the current SilipMunti features. It should be reviewed and customized by a qualified Philippine legal or privacy professional before a public production launch.

1. Scope and responsibility

This Privacy Policy applies to the SilipMunti website, accounts, rental listings, landlord verification, inquiries, messages, favorites, reviews, map features, and AI-assisted rental recommendations.

SilipMunti processes personal information in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and other applicable Philippine laws and regulations.

2. Information we collect

Account information

We may collect your first and last name, email address, phone number, password in hashed form, selected account role, profile picture, account status, and account activity dates.

Landlord and listing information

Landlords may provide property descriptions, prices, addresses, barangays, map coordinates, amenities, occupancy details, availability, photographs, and other information needed to create and manage rental listings.

Verification information

Landlords may submit a valid identification document, barangay clearance, and proof of property ownership or land title. These files may contain personal or sensitive personal information.

Platform activity and communications

We process inquiries, messages, favorites, reviews, feedback, notification status, AI recommendation prompts, and the property context connected to those interactions.

Technical information

We may receive IP address, browser and device information, timestamps, session identifiers, security events, error logs, and general usage information needed to operate and protect the platform.

3. How we use information

We may process information to:

  • Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure user accounts.
  • Review landlord accounts and verification documents.
  • Create, review, publish, update, and moderate listings.
  • Enable renter–landlord inquiries and messages.
  • Provide favorites, reviews, notifications, maps, and search.
  • Generate AI-assisted rental suggestions based on user preferences.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents.
  • Respond to support requests and enforce platform rules.
  • Comply with applicable laws, lawful orders, and regulatory duties.

Processing is performed using an applicable lawful basis, which may include consent, performance of a service requested by you, compliance with a legal obligation, or legitimate interests that do not override your rights.

4. How information is shared

SilipMunti does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed only as reasonably necessary in the following cases:

  • Public profile and listing information is displayed to website visitors as part of the rental marketplace.
  • Renters and landlords receive the information needed for their inquiries and conversations.
  • Authorized administrators access account, listing, review, and verification information for moderation and approval.
  • Hosting, email, security, map, and AI providers may process the limited information necessary to provide their services.
  • Information may be disclosed when required by law, legal process, public authority, or to protect users and the platform.

5. Landlord verification documents

Verification documents are intended for authorized administrative review and are not displayed publicly. A document contributes to full verification only after it is approved by an administrator.

Verified Landlord The landlord account is admin-approved but has fewer than three approved document types.
Fully Verified Landlord The landlord account and all three required document types have been approved.

Verification reduces certain platform risks but is not a guarantee of identity, ownership, property condition, transaction safety, or future conduct. Users must still exercise appropriate care before entering into a rental arrangement.

6. AI recommendations and map services

When you use AI-assisted recommendations, relevant preferences, prompts, and listing information may be sent to an AI service provider to generate a response. Do not include identification numbers, passwords, financial information, or other highly sensitive information in AI prompts or messages.

Map and location features may use third-party mapping services. Those providers may separately process device, network, or location information under their own privacy policies.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the platform, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, enforce agreements, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Retention periods may differ based on the information and purpose involved.

Deleted accounts or content may remain in restricted backups or records for a limited period when required for security, legal, or operational purposes.

8. Security and account protection

SilipMunti uses reasonable organizational and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including access controls, authentication, password hashing, session protection, restricted document access, and server-side validation.

No online system can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for protecting their login credentials and promptly reporting suspected unauthorized access.

9. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law and valid limitations, you may have the right to be informed, access your personal information, object to processing, request correction, request erasure or blocking, obtain data portability, withdraw consent where consent is the basis of processing, claim damages, and lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a privacy request. Some information may need to be retained when required by law or necessary for legitimate security and legal purposes.

10. Children’s privacy and external links

SilipMunti is intended for users who are at least 18 years old. The platform may contain links or integrations operated by third parties. Their services are governed by their own policies, and SilipMunti is not responsible for their independent practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the platform, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated date will be posted on this page, and material changes may also be communicated through the website or account notifications.
